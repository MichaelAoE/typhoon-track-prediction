Study on the optimal weighting impact of typhoon track and environmental
field in similar typhoon retrieval model for similar disasters

(1.Zhejiang Institute of Meteorological Sciences, Hangzhou 310008, Zhejiang, China;2.Zhejiang Branch, Chinese

Abstract
: [Objective] Similartyphoonretrievalmethodcanprovideimportantsupportfordisasterrisklossassessmentandemer-
gency decision. The typhoon-induced disaster is closely related not only to the track, but also to the factors determined by the
environmental field such as the intensity and the duration of the typhoon. In order to develop a set of similar typhoon model for
similar disasters, [Methods]firstly, the typhoon season is defined as June to November, the typhoons from 2014 to 2015 and
1981 to2015 are selected as the target samples and the retrieval samples respectively. The 500-hPa geopotential height field is
selectedtorepresenttheenvironmentalfield. Thesimilaritydeviationandtheoptimalsimilaritycoefficientisselectedtorepresent
the track and environmental field similarity,respectively. The two indexes are standardized and weighted to establish the similar
typhoon retrieval model. Secondly, basedontheretrievalmodel,theinfluenceofregionalandintensitydifferencesontheweight
of environmental field is investigated through the weighting test. Finally, typical case analysis and disaster pre-judgment and
interpretation are carried out for typhoon Champi (1525), Saomai (0608) and Lekima (1909) based on the data from 1949 to
2021. [Results] The results show that: (1) For typhoons in the Northwest Pacific, the distance deviation is relatively small
when the weight range of the environmental field is [0.3,0.8], and the direction deviation is relatively small when the weight
range is [0.2,0.7]. The optimal weights of both are around0.5. (2) For typhoons affecting China,when the weight range of
the environmental field is[0.5,0.9],thedistancedeviationanddirectiondeviationarerelativelysmall,andtheoptimalweight
is around0.6. (3) For typhoons of different intensities that affecting China, when the environmental field takes the optimal
weight, the improvementofTYs(Typhoons),STYs(SevereTyphoons) isthehighest. [Conclusion] Theintroductionoftheen-
vironmental field playsanimportantroleindeterminingthesimilartyphoon-induceddisasters. Theselectionoftheoptimalweight
of the environmentalfieldisrelatedtothelocationandintensityofthetyphoon. Thissimilartyphoonretrievalmodelhasacertain
role in the prediction of the turning track, and has a guiding significance for the prediction of the distribution and magnitude of
typhoon-induced disasters.
Keywords
: similar typhoon; environmental field; optimal weight; hazard; disaster assessment; precipitation; influencing
factors; climate change
